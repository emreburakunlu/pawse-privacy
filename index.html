Pawse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Pawse is a quit-smoking companion app with a virtual pet. Your privacy matters: Pawse does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Data stays on your device

Everything you enter, such as your pet's name, smoking logs, limits, points, purchases and preferences, is stored only on your device (and in an app group container shared with the Pawse widgets). It is never sent to us or to any third party.

No accounts, tracking or ads

Pawse has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

If you allow them, Pawse schedules reminders locally on your device. No notification content is sent to any server.

Health

Pawse is a motivational tool and does not provide medical advice. Please consult a healthcare professional for help quitting smoking.

Children

Pawse does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can erase all data in Settings, or by deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
